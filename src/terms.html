1. Coaching services

MS Performance Training offers personal training, strength and conditioning, group coaching and online programming, subject to availability and agreement on the service provided.

2. Booking and payment

Package inclusions, costs, payment frequency and minimum commitments will be explained and agreed before coaching begins. Any applicable taxes will be identified in the quoted price.

3. Cancellations and rescheduling

Clients must follow the notice period and catch-up conditions set out in their signed coaching agreement. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be forfeited in accordance with that agreement.

4. Health and participation

Clients should disclose relevant health concerns and exercise limitations before training. Coaching is not medical diagnosis or treatment. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional where appropriate.

5. Safety and conduct

Clients are expected to follow reasonable coaching and venue safety directions, use equipment responsibly and treat others respectfully.

6. Results

Individual results vary. No particular performance, weight or physique outcome is guaranteed.

7. Questions

For questions about your package or policies, please contact MSPT before purchasing.
