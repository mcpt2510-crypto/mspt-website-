About me

MORE THAN SETS AND REPS.

I'm Michel, a personal trainer and strength & conditioning coach based in Western Sydney. My background in football helped shape my passion for speed, strength, performance and the details that make an athlete better.

I work with young athletes, footballers and everyday people who want a clear plan and a coach who takes their progress seriously.

My approach is built on good movement, smart progressions, consistent effort and making sure you understand the reason behind the work.
