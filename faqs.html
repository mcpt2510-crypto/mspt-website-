Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Here are some of the things people ask before starting coaching with MSPT.

Do I need gym experience to start?

Not at all. Sessions can be adapted to your current strength, fitness and confidence level.

Do you train athletes as well as general gym clients?

Yes. Coaching includes athletic development, speed and strength work, as well as personal training for general fitness goals.

Where do sessions take place?

MSPT operates in Western Sydney. Contact me to confirm the available venue and session options for your location.

Do you offer online coaching?

Yes. Online programming and hybrid support may be available based on your goals.

How much does coaching cost?

Packages depend on session type and frequency. Get in touch for current rates and availability.

Can I train with a friend or in a group?

Yes. Small-group training may be an option. Send an enquiry with your group size.

What happens before my first session?

We discuss your goals, training experience, any relevant limitations and the most suitable starting point.

Can I reschedule a session?

Rescheduling and cancellation are subject to the policy agreed to at sign-up. Please review your coaching agreement for the applicable notice period.

Can you help with an injury?

Training can be modified where appropriate, but coaching is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
